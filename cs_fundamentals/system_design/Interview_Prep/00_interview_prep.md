## Quick Tips

### Before the Interview
1. Review common system design problems
2. Practice drawing diagrams
3. Memorize key estimation numbers
4. Know common design patterns

### During the Interview
1. **Clarify requirements** (5 min)
2. **Don't jump to solution** - understand the problem first
3. **Think out loud** - explain your reasoning
4. **Draw diagrams** - visual communication
5. **Discuss trade-offs** - every decision has pros/cons
6. **Check in with interviewer** - make it a conversation

### Key Phrases
```
"Before I start, let me clarify some requirements..."
"I'm assuming X. Does that sound right?"
"The trade-off here is..."
"Should I dive deeper into X or move on to Y?"
"One bottleneck I see is... and we can solve it by..."
```
