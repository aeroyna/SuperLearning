# Practice Problems: Recursion and Backtracking

Exploring all possibilities, pruning invalid paths.

## Subsets & Permutations

| Problem | Difficulty | Key Insight |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| [Subsets](https://leetcode.com/problems/subsets/) | Medium | Include/Exclude pattern or iteration. |
| [Permutations](https://leetcode.com/problems/permutations/) | Medium | Swap elements or use `used` array. |
| [Combination Sum](https://leetcode.com/problems/combination-sum/) | Medium | Unbounded choice (same index next recursion). |
