# Microsoft Math & Geometry Patterns

**Frequency**: ⚪ **Low/Medium**

While not as frequent as arrays or trees, Microsoft likes to ask problems that involve simple math, bit manipulation, or geometry. These often check for attention to detail (overflow, edge cases).

## Key Concepts
- **Modular Arithmetic**: Handling large numbers.
- **Bitwise Operations**: XOR, shifts, masking.
- **Geometry**: Lines, squares, rectangles, overlap.
- **Number Theory**: Primes, GCD, factors.
