# Adobe Trees & Graphs Patterns

**Frequency**: 🟠 **High**

Adobe frequently asks standard tree traversals and graph connectivity problems. They also value understanding of BST properties and recursion.

## Key Concepts
- **Recursion**: Essential for tree depth, inversion, and path sums.
- **BFS/DFS**: Level order traversal and connected components in grids.
- **BST Validation**: Checking properties.
- **LCA**: Finding common ancestors.
