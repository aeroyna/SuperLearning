# Adobe Dynamic Programming Patterns

**Frequency**: 🟠 **High**

Dynamic Programming is a favorite topic at Adobe, especially optimization problems involving grids, subsequences, and decision making (House Robber style).

## Key Concepts
- **1D DP**: Linear recurrence relations (Fibonacci style).
- **Grid DP**: Counting paths or finding min/max costs.
- **Knapsack**: Subset selection.
- **String DP**: Editing or matching strings.
