# Adobe Arrays & Strings Patterns

**Frequency**: 🔴 **Very High**

Adobe loves standard array and string manipulation problems. Efficiency and clean implementation of "Two Pointers" and "Sliding Window" are key.

## Key Concepts
- **Two Pointers**: Merging, partitioning, and finding pairs.
- **Sliding Window**: Substring problems (longest without repeating, etc.).
- **Hash Maps**: Counting frequencies, grouping anagrams.
- **Math/Simulation**: Integer conversion, water containers.
