# Google Arrays & Strings Patterns

**Frequency**: 🟠 **High**

These problems often serve as "warm-ups" or form the core logic of complex questions. Google focuses on efficiency (O(N) time, O(1) space) and handling edge cases.

## Key Concepts
- **Sliding Window**: Variable size windows for substrings/subarrays.
- **Two Pointers**: Meeting in the middle, or slow/fast pointers.
- **Prefix Sum**: Range queries or subarray sums.
- **Monotonic Stack**: Finding next greater/smaller elements.
