# Google Tree Patterns

**Frequency**: 🟡 **Medium**

Tree problems at Google often involve traversals, properties of BSTs, or path finding.

## Key Concepts
- **Recursion**: The heart of most tree problems.
- **Serialization**: Converting a tree to a string/array and back.
- **LCA (Lowest Common Ancestor)**: Finding the meeting point of two nodes.
- **Path Building**: Constructing paths between nodes.
