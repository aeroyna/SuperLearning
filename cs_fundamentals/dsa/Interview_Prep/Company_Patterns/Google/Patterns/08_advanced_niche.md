# Google Advanced & Niche Patterns

**Frequency**: ⚪ **Low (but High Signal)**

These patterns appear less frequently but often target experienced candidates or distinguish top performers. They require deeper understanding or specialized data structures.

## Key Concepts
- **Segment Tree / Fenwick Tree**: For efficient range queries and updates.
- **Rolling Hash**: For fast substring search and duplicate detection.
- **Minimax / Heuristic Search**: For game theory problems or complex search spaces.
