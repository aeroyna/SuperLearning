# Company-Specific Interview Patterns

Different companies have distinct interview styles and problem preferences. This guide covers what to expect at top tech companies.

## Overview

Each company values different aspects:
- **Google**: Algorithmic depth, scalability thinking
- **Amazon**: LP integration, practical coding
- **Microsoft**: Problem solving, communication
- **Meta**: Speed, optimization
- **Adobe**: Mixed difficulty, design
- **NVIDIA**: Systems, GPU-related
