# Amazon Stacks & Queues Patterns

**Frequency**: 🟡 **Medium**

Stacks and Queues are fundamental data structures that appear in various Amazon problems, from validating parentheses to processing data streams and implementing specific algorithms like BFS or DFS iteratively.

## Key Concepts
- **Stack**: LIFO (Last In, First Out). Used for parenthesis matching, expression evaluation, and backtracking.
- **Queue**: FIFO (First In, First Out). Essential for BFS, level-order traversals, and managing tasks.
- **Monotonic Stack/Queue**: Optimizing problems requiring "next greater/smaller element" or "sliding window maximum".
