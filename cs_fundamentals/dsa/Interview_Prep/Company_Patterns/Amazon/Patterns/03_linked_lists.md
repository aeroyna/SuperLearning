# Amazon Linked Lists Patterns

**Frequency**: 🟠 **High**

Linked List problems are fundamental at Amazon, testing your ability to handle pointer manipulation, manage memory, and solve problems efficiently with limited information.

## Key Concepts
- **Reversal**: Reversing full lists or sub-sections.
- **Two Pointers**: Fast/slow for cycle detection, middle finding, or Nth node from end.
- **Dummy Node**: Simplifying operations at the head of the list.
- **Deep Copy**: For lists with complex node structures (e.g., random pointers).
