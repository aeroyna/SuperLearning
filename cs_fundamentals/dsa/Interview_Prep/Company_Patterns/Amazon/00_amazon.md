# Amazon Interview Patterns (2024-2025)

Amazon's interviews are deeply rooted in foundational data structures, algorithms, and practical problem-solving. A significant emphasis is also placed on the company's Leadership Principles, which often influence behavioral questions.

## 📊 2024-2025 Trends & Shift

*   **Foundational DS&A**: Strong and consistent focus on core data structures (Arrays, Strings, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs, Hash Tables).
*   **Efficiency**: Problems consistently demand optimal time and space complexity, pushing candidates to apply techniques like Two Pointers, Sliding Window, and Heaps.
*   **Specific DP**: Dynamic Programming is common for optimization and combinatorial problems.
*   **Tree and Graph Traversals**: BFS/DFS are paramount, particularly for tree properties and grid problems (e.g., connected components, shortest paths).
*   **Design Hybrid**: Questions that blend algorithm implementation with class design (e.g., `LRU Cache`) are high frequency.

## 🧠 Amazon-Specific Tips

1.  **Leadership Principles (LPs)**: Be prepared to discuss your experiences related to Amazon's LPs in both behavioral and technical interviews.
2.  **Trade-offs**: Always discuss time and space complexity and be ready to explain trade-offs between different approaches.
3.  **Clarify First**: Amazon interviewers expect you to ask clarifying questions to understand constraints and edge cases fully.
4.  **Optimal Solution**: Aim for the most optimal solution, but start with a brute-force approach and optimize iteratively.
