## 🤝 Contributing to Chromium

This section outlines the process of submitting patches.

### 1. [Workflow](01_contribution_workflow.md)
*   **Checkout**: Creating a new branch.
*   **Upload**: `git cl upload`.
*   **Review**: Addressing feedback on Gerrit.
*   **Commit**: The commit queue (CQ).

### 2. [Coding Style](02_coding_standards.md)
*   **Coding Style**: Google C++ Style Guide.
*   **Testing**: Adding tests for your changes.
*   **Description**: Writing good CL descriptions.
