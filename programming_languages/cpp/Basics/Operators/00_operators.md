# Operators

Operators are the symbols that tell the compiler to perform specific mathematical or logical manipulations. C++ provides a rich set of built-in operators and allows you to define your own (operator overloading) for user-defined types. This chapter covers the syntax and semantics of these operators, including their precedence and associativity rules, which determine the order of evaluation in complex expressions.

You will learn about:
- **Arithmetic & Assignment:** performing calculations and updating values.
- **Relational & Logical:** Making comparisons and combining boolean logic.
- **Bitwise Operations:** Manipulating individual bits for low-level control and optimization.
- **Operator Precedence:** Understanding the hierarchy of operations to write correct and unambiguous expressions.
